Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Scope: This Privacy Policy applies to the Tabloop company website. Individual Tabloop products may have separate privacy policies that describe how those products handle data.

1. Who we are

Tabloop is an independent software studio operated as a sole proprietorship in India. We build privacy-first web and mobile products. In this policy, “Tabloop”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the sole proprietorship operating under the Tabloop trade name.

2. Our privacy approach

We aim to collect as little personal data as reasonably necessary. Where practical, our products favour local or on-device processing, local storage, minimal account requirements and transparent data practices. The Tabloop company website itself is primarily informational.

3. Information you provide

If you contact us through the website, we receive the information you submit, which may include your name, email address, subject and message. Please avoid including sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry.

4. Contact-form processing

The website uses Netlify Forms to receive contact messages. Form submissions are processed and stored by Netlify on our behalf so that we can review and respond to them. We use contact-form information only for handling enquiries, maintaining appropriate business records, preventing abuse, and meeting legal obligations where applicable.

5. Technical information and hosting

The website is hosted by Netlify. Like most hosting providers, Netlify may process limited technical information needed to deliver, secure and operate the site, such as IP address, browser or device information, request data, timestamps and security logs. This processing is handled under Netlify's own terms and privacy practices.

6. Fonts and external resources

The website currently loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font files, Google may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. We do not use Google Fonts for advertising or behavioural profiling.

7. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Tabloop does not currently use advertising trackers or behavioural analytics on this company website. We do not intentionally set marketing cookies. Essential technical mechanisms used by our hosting or security providers may still be involved in delivering and protecting the site. If we later introduce analytics or similar technologies, we will update this policy and provide any notice or choice required by applicable law.

8. How we use information

  • To respond to messages and enquiries.
  • To operate, secure and troubleshoot the website.
  • To prevent spam, misuse, fraud or security incidents.
  • To maintain limited business records where reasonably necessary.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and lawful requests.

9. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal data. We may share or allow processing of personal data only when reasonably necessary with service providers that help operate the website, such as Netlify and Google Fonts, or when required by law, legal process, or to protect our rights, users or systems.

10. International processing

Some service providers we use may process data outside India. Where this occurs, the processing is subject to the provider's contractual, security and privacy arrangements and any applicable legal requirements.

11. Data retention

We retain contact enquiries only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate business records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse or comply with applicable law. We may periodically delete form submissions that are no longer required.

12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect information handled through the website. However, no website, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have rights concerning your personal data, including rights to request access, correction, updating or deletion, and to raise a grievance. We will respond to valid requests in accordance with applicable law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 as their relevant provisions apply and come into force.

To make a privacy request, use the contact form on the Tabloop website and clearly state that your message concerns privacy or personal data.

14. Children's privacy

This company website is not designed to knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal data to us without appropriate permission, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

15. Product-specific privacy

Tabloop products may process different information or use different technical architectures. Product-specific privacy policies take precedence for the operation of those products.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, products, service providers or legal requirements change. The updated date at the top of this page will show when the policy was last revised.

17. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns, please use the contact form on the Tabloop website.
