Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This is a launch-ready placeholder for the Tabloop company website. Product-specific privacy policies should remain separate and reflect the actual behaviour of each product.

1. Our approach to privacy

Tabloop is built around a privacy-first philosophy. We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate our website and communicate with people who contact us.

2. Information you choose to provide

If you use our contact form, we may receive the name, email address, subject and message you submit. We use this information to respond to your enquiry.

3. Website hosting and technical data

Our hosting provider may process limited technical information required to deliver and secure the website, such as IP addresses, request logs and basic device or browser information. The exact handling depends on the hosting provider used for deployment.

4. Analytics and tracking

Tabloop does not intend to use invasive tracking. If privacy-respecting analytics are added in the future, this policy should be updated before or at the time those tools are enabled.

5. Product privacy

Tabloop products may have their own privacy policies because they can process different categories of information and may use different technical architectures. Please review the privacy policy provided with the relevant product.

6. Data retention

Contact enquiries may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, or meet applicable legal obligations.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, use the contact form on the Tabloop website.
