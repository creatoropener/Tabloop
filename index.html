About Tabloop

Building useful software without unnecessary data collection.

Tabloop is an independent software studio focused on building practical, privacy-first web and mobile products.

We started with a simple belief: useful software should not require people to give up more personal data than necessary.

Where practical, our products favour browser-based or on-device processing, local storage, minimal account requirements and transparent data practices.

Our core promise
Solve the problem.
Keep it simple.
Respect the user's data.
Built independently in India
Vision

Make useful software simpler, more accessible, and more private by design.

We envision a future where people do not have to trade privacy for convenience, and where respecting user data is a normal part of good software.

Mission

Build simple, reliable, privacy-first digital products that solve real everyday problems.

We focus on thoughtful design, useful functionality, transparent data practices and privacy-conscious technology.

How we build

Privacy isn't an extra feature.

It is part of the product decisions we make from the beginning.

01

Privacy first

We aim to minimise unnecessary data collection and favour local processing where practical.

02

Simple by design

Clear workflows and useful features matter more than complexity for its own sake.

03

Useful first

We build around real problems instead of adding features simply because we can.

04

Transparent

People should understand what a product does and how their information is handled.

05

User control

Where practical, users should remain in control of where their information is stored.

06

Always improving

We learn from real usage, feedback and changing technology to keep improving our products.

Our privacy philosophy

Your data doesn't need to be our business.

Where practical, Tabloop products are designed to process information locally, minimise data collection and keep users in control of their information.
